Dots & Boxes

Claim the last edge of a box to own it. Own the most boxes to win.

Rules:

Move: Draw one line between two dots
Claim: Complete a box's 4th side to own it
Bonus Turn: Completing a box gives you another turn
Win Condition: Own the most boxes when the board is full
Player 1: 0
Player 2: 0
Status: Player 1's turn
