Build a personal crisis plan you can lean on when things get hard. All data stays only on your device (browser storage). Nothing is sent to any server. This is not a substitute for professional care - if you are in immediate danger, call emergency services.
Important: Crisis resources - New Zealand

If you're feeling unsafe, in crisis, or experiencing thoughts of self-harm or suicide, please reach out immediately.

  • Emergency Services Call 111 if you are in immediate danger.
  • Need to Talk? Call or text 1737 - free, 24/7 counselling service in New Zealand.
  • Suicide Crisis Helpline Call 0508 828 865 (0508 TAUTOKO) - 24/7 specialist support.
  • Lifeline (NZ) Call 0800 543 354 or text 4357 - 24/7 support.
  • Healthline Call 0800 611 116 - health advice and triage (24/7).
  • Regional Crisis Resolution teams (urgent mental health assessment)
  • Christchurch Call 0800 920 092
  • Auckland Call 0800 800 717
  • Wellington / Porirua / Hutt Valley Call 0800 745 477
  • Waikato Call 0800 50 50 50
  • Otago Call 0800 467 846 (press 2)
  • Southland Call 0800 467 846 (press 1)
  • Young people
  • What's Up (kids & teens) Call 0800 942 8787 - phone/text/chat support.
  • Youthline Call 0800 376 633 or text 234. Online chat also available.
