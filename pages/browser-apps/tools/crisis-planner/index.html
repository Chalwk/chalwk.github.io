Crisis & support resources (New Zealand) tap to expand

If you're feeling unsafe, in crisis, or experiencing thoughts of self-harm or suicide, please reach out immediately. All numbers are free to call from NZ phones.

  • Emergency Services Call 111 if you are in immediate danger.
  • Need to Talk? Call or text 1737 - free, 24/7 counselling.
  • Suicide Crisis Helpline Call 0508 828 865 (0508 TAUTOKO) - 24/7.
  • Lifeline (NZ) Call 0800 543 354 or text 4357 - 24/7.
  • Healthline Call 0800 611 116 - health advice & triage (24/7).
